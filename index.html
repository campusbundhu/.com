About Us

CampusBandhu is a student-first platform designed to simplify college life in Jalgaon and surrounding educational hubs. We understand the struggles of finding a good PG, getting affordable notes, or locating quality food mess.

Our mission is to connect students directly with verified owners and peers in their locality without high brokerage fees or obsolete database record clutter.

Our Vision

To create a transparent, zero-brokerage, student-focused ecosystem empowering every student to access essentials seamlessly.
