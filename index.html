About Us

CampusBandhu is a student-first platform designed to simplify college life in Jalgaon. We understand the struggles of finding a good PG, getting affordable notes, or finding the right mess.

Our mission is to connect students with verified services in their locality, ensuring a hassle-free campus experience.

Our Vision

To create a zero-brokerage, student-run ecosystem that empowers every student in Jalgaon to find the resources they need instantly.
